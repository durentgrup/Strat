## 1. Trend summary by theme

### A. Event industry: bazaar / campus / expo (PRIORITY)

**A1. Headline growth vs. a split market.** The Indonesian exhibition/MICE industry is projected to grow ~15% in 2026 against ~5% national GDP growth ([Metro TV](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/kpLCQ7MY-industri-mice-diprediksi-naik-15-persen-di-2026), [Infobank](https://infobanknews.com/industri-mice-indonesia-diproyeksikan-tumbuh-15-persen-pada-2026)). IVENDO's national survey counted **8,777 events across 34 provinces worth Rp 84.46 trillion in 2024**, employing 8.8 million people ([IVENDO survey 2024–2025](https://ivendo.or.id/hasil-survei-industri-event-nasional-2024-2025/)). Government supported 134 events in 2025 drawing 10.8M visitors; Kemenpar targets MICE forex contribution rising from 10% to 15% by 2029 ([Marketing.co.id](https://marketing.co.id/mice-jadi-pilar-pertumbuhan-ekonomi-indonesia-2026/)).

**A2. BUT: government-driven MICE is collapsing under budget efficiency.** Inpres 01/2025 (belanja negara/daerah efficiency) has gutted government meetings/ceremonial spending: MICE players report performance declines of **60–65% by Q1 2026** (vs 35–40% decline in 2025), with some East Java MICE firms cutting **up to 70% of workers** ([Kompas.id](https://www.kompas.id/artikel/en-dampak-efisiensi-anggaran-pemerintah-industri-mice-pangkas-pekerja-hingga-70-persen), [beritajatim](https://beritajatim.com/sektor-ekonomi-kreatif-jawa-timur-di-titik-nadir-38-juta-pekerja-terancam-akibat-efisiensi-anggaran)). Central transfers to regions are cut **~25% in 2026 vs 2025** ([PHRI](https://phri.or.id/media/detail/5795), [Bisnis Sumsel, 22 Jun 2026](https://sumatra.bisnis.com/read/20260622/534/1982520/hotel-sumsel-tertekan-efisiensi-anggaran-phri-dorong-geliat-mice)). Jakarta hotels/venues reliant on government activity are hit hardest ([VenueMagz/Colliers 2026 outlook](https://venuemagz.com/news/proyeksi-colliers-indonesia-terhadap-kinerja-hotel-di-tahun-2026/)).
**Implication for Durent:** the "GOR/pemda bazaar venue" lead lane and APKASI-adjacent regional-government budgets carry real 2026 budget risk — regional pemda discretionary spend is squeezed. Private/consumer-facing events (bazaars, music fests, brand expos, campus events) are the resilient side of the split market. Weight pipeline accordingly.

**A3. Consumer/creative bazaars are growing strongly.** Jakarta Kreatif Festival 2026 (Istora Senayan, 4–5 Jul 2026, Bank Indonesia DKI) hit **Rp 55 billion transactions with 377 tenants** vs Rp 21 billion at JKF 2025 ([Kompas.id](https://www.kompas.id/artikel/jakarta-kreatif-festival-2026-suguhkan-pameran-umkm-musik-film-hingga-olahraga), [romisaputra.com, Jul 2026](https://www.romisaputra.com/2026/07/jakarta-kreatif-festival-2026.html)). Government ministries themselves run UMKM bazaars (Kemendagri Festival Bazar, 12–14 Aug 2026, 57 tenants — [IDN Times](https://www.idntimes.com/business/economy/kemendagri-festival-bazar-edukatif-02-sk72n-r8ty07)). Dedicated bazaar operators like [BazaarJakartaID](https://bazaarjakarta.id/) run continuous mall/venue bazaar circuits — exactly Durent's ICP (organizers needing turnkey tenda/HT/equipment).

**A4. Campus season is live right now.** PKKMB 2026 runs mid-August (UI: 3–18 Aug; UPNVJ FISIP: 12–13 Aug; Gunadarma, STIA LAN etc. similar) with lectures starting late Aug–early Sep ([kumparan](https://kumparan.com/berita-hari-ini/kapan-pkkmb-2026-ini-jadwal-ui-ugm-hingga-ub-27t5CaPP1CT), [Tribunnews](https://www.tribunnews.com/pendidikan/7846816/panduan-pkkmb-2026-materi-durasi-kegiatan-aturan-hingga-larangan-yang-wajib-diketahui-maba)). **PKKMB 2026 is largely done/finishing — the next campus wave is the Sep–Dec festival season**: pensi, faculty fests, Pop Up Market (Prasetiya Mulya), Jazz Goes to Campus (UI Depok, traditionally late Nov), and similar recurring student events ([helloswag](https://www.helloswag.id/blog/event-mahasiswa/), [infopensi.com](https://infopensi.com/)). Outreach for PKKMB should target the 2027 cycle (committees form ~Q2); near-term campus revenue = Sep–Dec fests.

**A6. For a support/rental vendor this mostly means: more outdoor/semi-outdoor activations (tenda demand), more multi-zone layouts (HT/comms demand), and tighter production timelines (premium on reliable one-stop vendors).

### B. Film / commercial production support & rental (brief — pivot de-risking)

- **H1 2026 cinema admissions fell 16.7% YoY** (53.77M viewers Jan–Jun; March/Lebaran was the peak at 11.84M) ([Republika](https://ameera.republika.co.id/berita/tj3bl9425/penonton-bioskop-indonesia-turun-167-persen-pada-paruh-pertama-2026)).
- Local films still hold ~60% of box office and 2026 has produced 12 titles past 1M admissions with genre diversification beyond horror ([Kompas](https://www.kompas.com/hype/read/2026/05/29/211140066/daftar-12-film-indonesia-tembus-1-juta-penonton-di-bioskop-tahun-2026?page=all), [Media Indonesia](https://mediaindonesia.com/hiburan/883378/9-film-indonesia-tembus-1-juta-penonton-2026-horor-masih-dominan)).
- Structural constraint: too few screens → local films "cannibalize" each other ([Hypeabis](https://hypeabis.id/read/52028/jumlah-layar-minim-film-indonesia-saling-kanibal-di-bioskop)). Production volume is healthy but budgets face soft-audience pressure.
- **Read for strategy:** the film line is stable-but-flat with softening H1 demand; nothing found suggests a near-term surge in production-support day-rate demand. Combined with Durent's own data (production houses have direct vendor relations; cold outreach = zero leads), the research **supports keeping film as a maintain/warm-referral line and concentrating growth effort on events.**

### C. Regulatory, economic & industry-body news

- **Budget efficiency (Inpres 01/2025) is THE dominant policy force** on the event industry in 2026 — see A2. IVENDO has formally lobbied for relaxation of the efficiency policy on government event spending ([Bisniswisata](https://bisniswisata.co.id/ivendo-harapkan-kelonggaran-pada-kebijakan-efisiensi-anggaran-belanja-pemerintah/)).
- **Purchasing power is soft:** middle class shrank to ~46.7M (2025) from 47.9M (2024); households are in "defensive mode"; consumer/retail consumption growth projected only ~3% vs 5% GDP ([Kompas money, 2 Jun 2026](https://money.kompas.com/read/2026/06/02/130400226/daya-beli-melemah-dan-masa-depan-kelas-menengah?page=all), [beritasatu](https://www.beritasatu.com/bplus/2994037/ekonomi-naik-daya-beli-terjepit)). Yet mass-market shopping festivals still perform (Indonesia Shopping Festival 2026: 7–23 Aug, 407 malls — [Antara](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5684715/appbi-isf-2026-bantu-dongkrak-daya-beli-masyarakat-menengah-bawah)) — value-oriented bazaar formats fit the moment.
- **Tax:** concert/entertainment tickets sit under regional PBJT (typically 10%, up to 25–35% in some regions), not the 12% PPN; PPN 12% instead hits inputs/merchandise ([Kompas.id](https://www.kompas.id/artikel/imbas-ppn-12-persen-harga-tiket-konser-musik-akan-terkerek-naik), [hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/netflix--pulsa--tiket-pesawat-dan-tiket-konser-tetap-dikenai-ppn-12-persen-begini-penjelasan-djp-lt676a474797468/)). Minor cost pressure for organizers; no direct barrier for Durent.
- **Industry bodies:** IVENDO signed an MoU with Kemenparekraf to strengthen the national event ecosystem ([merdeka/planet](https://planet.merdeka.com/lifestyle/kolaborasi-ivendo-kemenparekraf-perkuat-ekosistem-event-nasional-ini-strategi-bersaing-global-454084-mvk.html)); ASPERAPI hosted AFECA/A20/IBEF (Nov 2025, JIExpo) and holds Rakernas 2026 in Labuan Bajo; IBEM 2026 launches at JICC as Indonesia's first MICE buyer–seller marketplace ([Journey of Indonesia](https://journeyofindonesia.com/events/ibem-2026-marketplace-pertama-industri-mice-indonesia-yang-pertemukan-ratusan-buyers-dan-sellers/)). Joining/monitoring IVENDO or ASPERAPI channels is a cheap way into organizer deal flow.
- **IFFa (Indonesia Film Facilitation, PFN×Telkom/Nuon location-permit portal, iffa.id): no substantive news found since its 2023–24 launch coverage** ([Antara 2023](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/3821268/pfn-rilis-situs-web-resmi-iffa-mudahkan-perizinan-lokasi-untuk-sineas)) — consistent with the internal read that this government-side competitor to Durent's location line is **dormant**. White space in "Location Dept as a Service" remains open, but it is a film-segment asset, secondary to the pivot.

### D. Broader strategic signals worth flagging

1. **Vendor-side capacity is loosening.** With government-MICE players cutting staff/idle, subvendor capacity (tenda, sound, crew) should be easier and possibly cheaper to lock in — good timing for Durent's written vendor-partnership + rate-card push, and for recruiting hungry subvendors before the Q4 season.
2. **Demand is bifurcating: private up, government down.** Prioritize brand/consumer bazaars, campus fests, pop-culture cons, corporate year-end events; treat pemda/GOR and APKASI-follow-on regional budgets as slower, tender-driven money (Durent's PT registration is the right unlock for that lane, but don't stake the Dec 2026 cash target on it).
3. **Venue landscape shifting:** Gaikindo is weighing moving GIIAS from ICE BSD to a new PIK venue ([Katadata](https://auto.katadata.co.id/news/gaikindo-has-not-yet-decided-to-move-giias-from-ice-bsd-to-pik-19858)); PIK2 is emerging as a third Jabodetabek event hub (Comic Con 3–4 Oct). Worth adding PIK2 organizers to the target list alongside ICE BSD/JIExpo.
4. **EO pricing up 10–15% YoY** amid input inflation — supports Durent holding rate-card pricing rather than discounting ([kiakrikil](https://kiakrikil.com/daftar-harga-event-organizer/)).
5. **Near-term timing:** AOE 2026 is 27–29 Aug (next week). It is Durent's single most time-critical warm opportunity — on-site presence/servicing there feeds both immediate revenue and the regional-government relationship lane for 2027.
