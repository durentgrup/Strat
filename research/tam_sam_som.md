# TAM / SAM / SOM — Durent Support (Event Segment)

**Prepared:** 2026-09-03 · **Scope:** Physical event-production support (tenda, sound, HT/comms, kursi-meja, catering, crew, transport) for bazaar/campus/expo organizers in Jabodetabek — Durent's current pivot focus. Film/commercial line is treated separately at the end (it's explicitly a "maintain" line, not a growth target).

**Read this before the numbers.** A TAM/SAM/SOM asked to be "akurat" and "jujur" has a specific failure mode: multiplying three or four *assumed* percentages together (X% of GDP × Y% is events × Z% is production spend × W% is Jabodetabek) produces a number with the *appearance* of precision and none of the substance — the errors compound multiplicatively and the output can be off by 10-50x with no way to tell. So this document does the top-down version anyway (for context, because it's the version most brand decks show), but the number you should actually trust is the **bottom-up SAM** — built by literally counting venues/organizers Durent could realistically sell to and multiplying by Durent's own real price list. Where I'm estimating rather than citing a hard number, I say so explicitly.

---

## 1. TAM — Total Addressable Market

### 1a. The industry-level ceiling (top-down, context only)
- **Rp 84.46 trillion** — total value of Indonesia's event industry in 2024, across **8,777 events in 34 provinces**, employing 8.8 million people. Source: [IVENDO National Event Industry Survey 2024–2025](https://ivendo.or.id/hasil-survei-industri-event-nasional-2024-2025/), a survey of 90 respondents across 16 provinces run with 7 partner associations (ASPPI, PaSKI, ASITA, HASTANA, AELI, KADIN, APMI).
- This number is **everything**: venue rental, artist/talent fees, F&B, ticketing, marketing, government ceremonial spend — the entire event economy. It is not a number Durent can meaningfully "address." It's here only so the smaller numbers below have a ceiling to be sanity-checked against.
- DKI Jakarta is confirmed as Indonesia's **#1 MICE/event destination** (ahead of Bali, Yogyakarta, West Java, East Java) per the same survey — but **no province-level Rp breakdown is published**. Any Jakarta/Jabodetabek % you see below is my estimate, not IVENDO's data.

### 1b. Narrowing to "production & logistics support" (the category Durent actually sells)
Using an Indonesian corporate-event budgeting benchmark ([agievent.com](https://agievent.com/news/budget-event-perusahaan), [Evoria](https://evoriaevent.com/estimasi-biaya-event/)), a typical event budget splits roughly:

| Line item | % of budget | Durent-relevant? |
|---|---|---|
| Venue & lokasi | 25–40% | No (Durent doesn't sell venue) |
| Konsumsi & catering | 20–35% | Partially (Durent has a catering line, but competes with dedicated caterers) |
| **Produksi & dekorasi** (stage, sound, lighting, backdrop, signage) | **10–20%** | **Yes — this is Durent's core** |
| Talent/entertainment | 15–30% | No |
| Dokumentasi | 5–10% | Partially (Durent offers this at Rp8jt/day) |
| **Logistik & transportasi** | **10–25%** | **Yes** |
| Contingency | 5–10% | No |

Taking just the two lines Durent squarely owns (produksi 10–20% + logistik 10–25%, using roughly half of logistik since not every event buys external transport) gives **~15–25% of total event value** as the realistic "production support" slice, midpoint ~18%.

**Caveat that matters:** this benchmark is built for **corporate/MICE events**. Durent's actual ICP — bazaars, campus fests, community events — has almost no "talent fee" or big venue-rental line; the event basically *is* the tenda+sound+booth infrastructure. So for Durent's real customers, production/logistics is likely a **higher** share of a **much smaller** total budget per event than this corporate benchmark implies. I flag this rather than paper over it.

### 1c. National → Jabodetabek
No published %. Reasoned estimate based on: Jakarta hosts the 3 flagship national venues (JCC, JIExpo, ICE BSD), Jabodetabek holds **~150 of Indonesia's ~400 shopping malls** (~90 in Jakarta + ~60 in Bogor/Depok/Tangerang/Bekasi — [idxchannel](https://www.idxchannel.com/economics/indonesia-miliki-lebih-dari-400-mal-paling-banyak-di-jawa), [jadipunya.id](https://www.jadipunya.id/peta-mall-jakarta-jabodetabek/)), and Jabodetabek is ~25–30% of national GDP. I use **25–35% of national event value**, midpoint 30% — **this is my assumption, not a cited fact.**

### TAM, stacked (all estimate-dependent past step 1a):
Rp84.46T × 18% (production slice) × 30% (Jabodetabek share) ≈ **Rp 4.6 trillion/year**, with a defensible range of **Rp 2.3T – Rp 8.4T** depending on which end of each range you take.

**This number is not useful for decision-making.** It's three compounding assumptions deep. It tells you the industry is "big enough" and nothing else. Treat it as a ceiling, not a target.

## 2. SAM — Serviceable Addressable Market (the number to actually trust)

Built bottom-up: count Jabodetabek organizers matching Durent's own stated ICP (**"organizer yang TIDAK punya vendor network sendiri"** — per `durent-context.md` §5, explicitly excluding production houses and professional exhibition organizers who already have retained vendor relationships), times Durent's own real price list (from `Proposal Event support.pdf`: sound 5000W = Rp4.5jt, HT 20-unit/day = Rp250k, catering Rp60-120k/pax, tenda packages, etc. — a realistic full bundle for a mid-size bazaar runs roughly **Rp8–15 juta**, and for a larger multi-day activation **Rp30–50+ juta**, consistent with the external benchmark of an 18 juta tenda-only job at a South Jakarta UMKM expo and a Rp45 juta multi-day wedding-scale roder job — see `research/competitor_analysis.md` §on pricing).

| Segment | Count/year (Jabodetabek) | Basis | Avg. ticket (full package) | Segment value/year |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mall-based bazaars | ~400 | ~150 malls, ~60% run outdoor/vendor-needing activations, avg. ~4/year each | Rp 8–15jt (mid Rp11.5jt) | **~Rp 4.6 miliar** |
| Campus events (PKKMB, faculty fests, pensi, dies natalis) | ~150 | ~50 active universities × ~3 major events/year | Rp 5–10jt (mid Rp7.5jt) | **~Rp 1.1 miliar** |
| Independent/community bazaars (kelurahan festivals, expat-community events, religious/holiday bazaars, UMKM ministry-run bazaars) | ~300–600 | Residual from IVENDO's implied Jabodetabek event count after removing mall+campus+large-flagship | Rp 5–15jt (mid Rp10jt) | **~Rp 4.5 miliar** |
| Large flagship events winnable by an outside vendor (community-run festivals, Pestapora-scale events *not* already vendor-locked) | ~6/year (≈30% of ~20 flagship-scale events) | Judgment call — most big trade expos (GIIAS, Mining Indonesia, etc.) have retained contractors and are **excluded** | Rp 30–50jt (mid Rp40jt) | **~Rp 0.3 miliar** |
| **Total SAM** | | | | **~Rp 10.5 miliar/year** |
| Range (low/high assumptions) | | | | **Rp 6–16 miliar/year** |

**This is the total revenue pool split across every vendor competing for it** — `competitor_analysis.md` names 15-20+ direct competitors just in Jabodetabek tenda/sound/HT rental (Over Organizer, Sewa Tenda Roder, Tenda Intan Ali, Seventent, Akusewa, Zein Event, and more), likely 50-100+ including small/unlisted operators. Rp10.5 miliar spread across that many competitors is a **fragmented, low-differentiation commodity market** — consistent with `competitor_analysis.md`'s own finding that most competitors compete on price transparency, not brand.

**Honest note on the gap:** the bottom-up SAM (~Rp10.5 miliar) is **~400x smaller** than the top-down "TAM" (~Rp4.6 trillion) from section 1. That gap is itself the finding: the top-down number was measuring the wrong thing (the whole event economy, most of which Durent structurally cannot touch — venue fees, talent, corporate MICE agencies with locked-in vendors). The bottom-up number is what's actually reachable.

## 3. SOM — Serviceable Obtainable Market (what Durent can realistically win in the next 12 months)

This is **not** "our target market share of SAM" picked arbitrarily — it's built from Durent's own real numbers first, then checked against a market-share logic as a sanity cross-check.

### Ground truth from Durent's own data (`durent-context.md` §10-11):
- 15 months of operation (mostly film-era, small-ticket): **Rp 191 juta total revenue**, ~Rp 12.7 juta/month average.
- Q1 2026 (event pivot starting to show): **Rp 55.4 juta from 11 clients** in the quarter — **~Rp 18.5 juta/month**, a real acceleration over the 15-month average.
- Average job **Rp 1.7 juta** — below every specialist competitor's minimum order (Rp 2.5–5 juta). `competitor_analysis.md` calls this explicitly a **ticket-size problem, not a volume problem**.
- Two deals of Rp 50jt+ and Rp 100jt+ nearly closed and didn't — not on price or execution, but because the organizer changed policy or the committee disappeared. This is real evidence the offer itself works at that ticket size; the failure mode is prospect follow-through, not product-market fit.
- Stated internal target: **Rp 100 juta net cash by end of 2026** (Rp50jt for a location move + Rp50jt runway) — i.e., leadership's own working number for "obtainable this year" is already in the same order of magnitude as the estimate below.

### Three scenarios (event segment, gross revenue, next 12 months):

| Scenario | Logic | Gross revenue/year |
|---|---|---|
| **Conservative** | Q1 2026 run-rate (Rp18.5jt/month) holds flat, no strategic change | **~Rp 150–220 juta** |
| **Base / realistic** | Executes the already-identified fix (raise ticket size via bundled packages instead of single-item orders per `competitor_analysis.md` §"raise deal size"), converts a handful of Rp15-40jt bundle deals instead of Rp1.7jt piecemeal jobs, no major capital raise | **~Rp 300–500 juta** |
| **Stretch** | Capital secured for an owned HT fleet (small capex, highest utilization per `competitor_analysis.md`), PT registration unlocks tender/institutional access, wins 2-3 flagship-scale events (Pestapora-class) | **~Rp 600 juta – 1 miliar** |

Cross-check against SAM: base case (~Rp400jt midpoint) is **~4% of the Rp10.5 miliar bottom-up SAM** — a believable share for a ~15-month-old bootstrapped entrant against a fragmented field with no dominant player, not a number that requires implausible market dominance.

### The honest bottleneck
Market size is **not** the constraint here — Rp10.5 miliar/year of addressable demand against Durent's Rp200-500jt obtainable range leaves enormous headroom. The constraints that actually cap SOM, per Durent's own documented reality:
1. **Ticket size**, not lead volume — 100+ cold emails got ~12 replies, proving demand exists; the problem is closing at Rp1.7jt/job instead of Rp15-40jt/bundle.
2. **Sales follow-through** — biggest near-misses died to prospect-side flakiness (policy changes, vanishing committees), which a longer nurture cadence (already identified internally) mitigates but doesn't eliminate.
3. **Capital for fleet ownership** (HT units especially) is explicitly constrained by the same Rp100jt cash target it would help hit — a chicken-and-egg the stretch scenario depends on resolving.
4. **Margin risk on bundled jobs**: the 56% gross margin figure is from the old (mostly film, small-ticket) revenue mix. `competitor_analysis.md` flags that Durent's tenda/kursi pricing implies a ~2.3x markup over publicly-listed upstream rates (Over Organizer publishes Rp25-60k/m² for tents) — margin on larger bundled event jobs, once organizers can price-check commodity line items themselves, may compress below 56%. The Rp-value scenarios above are revenue, not profit, for this reason.

## Summary (the numbers, plainly)

- **TAM (industry ceiling, not addressable):** ~Rp 84 trillion nationally / a rough, assumption-heavy **~Rp 4.6 trillion/year** if narrowed to "Jabodetabek production support spend" — treat as context, not a target.
- **SAM (the real, countable market):** **~Rp 10-11 miliar/year** across all competing vendors in Jabodetabek's bazaar/campus/community-event segment.
- **SOM (Durent, next 12 months, event segment):** realistically **Rp 150-500 juta**, with **Rp 600 juta - 1 miliar** possible only if capital + a couple of big-ticket wins land. The internal Rp100jt target is achievable at the low end of this without any strategic change; the upside requires actually fixing the ticket-size problem the team has already correctly diagnosed.
